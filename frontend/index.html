Where does NRW need more EV charging?

This project compares 53 districts in North Rhine-Westphalia to identify gaps in public charging, shortlist areas for new stations, and assess relative readiness for further electrification.

We compare charging supply with population and accessibility, then consider traffic, mapped electricity infrastructure and renewable energy. Select a question below, explore the map and ranking, and test a proposed station.

Measures
EV Readiness combines density (40%), accessibility (30%) and population-adjusted coverage (30%). Investment Priority combines charging deficit (60%) with infrastructure opportunity (40%).

Sources
Bundesnetzagentur charging stations; Eurostat population and boundaries; Straßen.NRW traffic; Energieatlas NRW energy data; OpenStreetMap grid and road data. See indicator formulas.

These are comparative screening scores, not forecasts or approved construction sites. Exact locations need checks for demand, land access and grid connection. Accessibility is a distance proxy, not a driving-time measurement.

How the indicators are calculated

All data sources

The canonical PostGIS calculation normalizes input values against fixed baseline 5th and 95th percentile bounds, and combines them using the weights below. Local fallback scores may use a simplified calculation; check the data notice above the map. Scenario stations change district values without moving the baseline comparison scale.

EV Readiness

40% Charger Density + 30% Charger Accessibility + 30% Population-adjusted Coverage.

Based on official/proposed station locations, distance from the district centroid, district area and Eurostat population.

Charger Deficit

Charger Deficit = 100 − EV Readiness.

A higher value identifies a larger relative charging-infrastructure gap.

Infrastructure Opportunity

40% Transport Load + 35% Grid Readiness Proxy + 25% Renewable Context.

Based on Straßen.NRW traffic, mapped OSM power infrastructure and Energieatlas NRW renewable assets.

Investment Priority

60% Charger Deficit + 40% Infrastructure Opportunity.

Ranks areas where the charging gap and supporting infrastructure context jointly justify closer planning review.

Grid Absorption Risk Proxy

45% Local Energy Balance + 30% Renewable Growth + 25% inverse Grid Readiness Proxy.

This is a screening proxy, not measured DSO hosting capacity. Feeder loading, transformer headroom and connection queues are not available.
